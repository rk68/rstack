---
name: grilling
description: Interview the user about a plan or design in batched question rounds until no open decision is left. Use when the user wants to stress-test a plan before building, or says 'grill' or 'grill me'.
---

Interview me about this plan until we share one understanding and no open decision is left. Walk each branch of the design tree, and settle decisions in the order their dependencies set. For each question, give your recommended answer.

Ask in rounds. Each round is one numbered list of every decision that is open now. Give each question one paragraph of plain-English context, lettered options, your recommendation and its main drawback. Wait for the answers, then repeat any unanswered question in a separate list before the next round. Ask a question that depends on an earlier answer in a later round.

If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase, explore the codebase instead.
